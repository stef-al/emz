previous emz (file coded by emcm as an opaque message), xz and zstd.  Every
emz archive was decompressed and compared with cmp (and emz's SHA-256 check).

Mode: a store in which data blocks that can be decoded make up less than 10%
of the file (both OSTs: 0.7% and 2.8%) keeps every block in place in the
skeleton; there the property models lost 0.4% to the coder finding blocks
next to their older copies in free space.  Both modes were measured:
  mtnman.ost            blocks modelled 2,523,706   kept in place 2,512,430
  pstextractortest.ost  blocks modelled 1,544,451   kept in place 1,538,811

Outlook's deflate: 40 compressed OST blocks were recompressed with zlib at
every level 0-9, window 9-15 bits, memLevel 7-9 and strategies 0-3; no setting
reproduces any of them, and the output differs from the third or fourth byte
on.  In size it matches zlib -9 (all 641 blocks of mtnman.ost: 866,625 bytes
against 865,001; per block -6.9% to +0.8%), so it is another deflate
implementation of similar strength.

Tried and dropped: decoding the permute encoding in free space (52,405 ->
52,946 bytes coded, per-64-byte choice 54,377); block IDs and offsets of
block B-tree leaves as differences (-0.3% on enron_noatt.pst, +0.07% on an
OST because old copies of the pages in free space stop matching); free space
as zero runs instead of raw bytes (+0.3% to +1.3%: it cuts up UTF-16 text).
